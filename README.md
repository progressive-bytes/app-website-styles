# BudgetBear

The private savings tracker that tells you when you can afford each purchase.

BudgetBear turns your wishlist into a clear timeline. Add your goals and their prices, prioritize what you want to buy first, and plan your deposits or withdrawals – BudgetBear calculates the earliest date you can afford each one, so you always know when your next target becomes affordable.

## Key Features

- Priority-based wishlists – add goals with estimated prices and drag them into your exact order of preference
- Dynamic timeline calculation – purchase dates update instantly as you adjust prices, add deposits, or reorder goals
- Flexible cash flow – add single deposits or log withdrawals at any time
- Recurring, finite deposit or withdrawal schedules to keep your timeline automated
- Group related goals into projects for travel, electronics, household upgrades, or emergency reserves
- Smart allocation – funds go toward your highest-priority goal first, then roll over to the next
- 100% local – no accounts, no servers, no trackers
- Clean, distraction-free interface

Whether it's sinking funds, a priority wishlist, or a major purchase broken into contributions, BudgetBear takes the guesswork out of saving and tells you exactly when you can buy.
